### **Towards Long Term Success**

If you have been in sales or business development in the tech industry, or if you work as consultant in the tech industry, you might have been exposed to the rhetoric of ***“you need to become a trusted advisor”*** for the customer.

The fact is **being a *trusted advisor* is not enough.** Let's start by considering the role of a *trusted advisor* versus the role of a *strategic advisor*. A *trusted advisor* supports a **previously established** client’s plan. These advisors are recognized as a reliable resources and problem solvers for a tactical challenge or set of challenges in the scope of a particular client’s plan.

From the client’s perspective, these *trusted advisors* are the ones the clients sees that can deliver a solution on time. This might seems like a good position to be in, but it only leads to tactical transactions. **If you don’t work towards the next level of trust** with your customer, **you’ll end up boxed or defined by tactical projects** you did for them. **In that case you have your client’s trust as *trusted advisors* but not as *strategic advisor***. So, what’s the difference?

**Becoming a *strategic advisor* is earning the next level of trust.** There is mutual respect and your services and products are seen as key elements for the success of the client’s strategic plans over the long run. At this stage, you have developed a good understanding of their business, business objectives and business challenges. If you are in sales, this is where you have developed the ability to present the tangible value of your products and services and how they map to your client’s strategic plans.

**As *strategic advisor* you will enjoy frequent participation in client’s most important plans.** You have earned your place as part of the few key individuals the client consult about their long term strategies. Now you support them **providing perspective, focus and insights for their next big plans.** You help them achieve measurable success. Even better, **you become part of their secret for success.** That is not something that can be replaced overnight. **If you are in any kind of sales or consulting role, this is the level to strive to be in.**

Individuals that achieve this role, forge long lasting relationships with their clients. **This type of advisors provide true business value to their clients.** In return, they receive a continuous flow of business opportunities with the participation in the strategic projects.

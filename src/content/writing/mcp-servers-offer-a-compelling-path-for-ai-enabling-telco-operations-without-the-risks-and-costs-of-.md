MCP Servers offer a compelling path for AI-enabling Telco operations without the risks and costs of replacing legacy OSS/BSS systems. While traditional OSS/BSS architectures weren't designed for modern intent-based approaches, they do expose northbound APIs that MCP Servers can leverage as intelligent translation layers. By sitting between AI applications and legacy systems, MCP Servers can translate high-level, intent-based requests into the specific API sequences that OSS/BSS systems understand, while encapsulating operational knowledge and complexity. This approach allows operators to protect their substantial existing investments, maintain operational continuity on proven systems, and begin realizing AI benefits immediately rather than waiting years for complete system modernization. It's an evolutionary rather than revolutionary path that respects both the technical realities of legacy infrastructure and the business imperative to adopt AI capabilities quickly and safely.

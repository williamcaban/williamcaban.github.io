A three-layer validity model showing how evaluation pipelines degrade across task generation, human-simulator calibration, and automated judgment stages — a pipeline retaining 70% validity at each stage is at most 34% valid overall. An analysis of 55 agentic evaluation papers found approximately 82% lack proper inter-rater reliability metrics. Identifies validity failures in popular benchmarks and proposes eight prescriptions grounded in psychometric science.
